UO Graduate Student Network

A chat network for grad students at the University of Oregon.

Join

  1. Open the browser chat, pick a nick, and press Start. You'll land in #help.
  2. Make an account with your UO email. Fill these in, then copy the line into the chat box and press Enter.
    /msg NickServ REGISTER yourpassword duckid@uoregon.edu
    This only fills in the line; nothing is sent anywhere.
  3. Check your UO email for a message from noreply@uogradnet.xyz. Copy the line in it that starts with /msg NickServ VERIFY into the chat box.
  4. Done. Join any channel below. Next time, tick "I have a password" on the start screen and you're logged in.

Using your own IRC client? Connect to irc.uogradnet.xyz, port 6697, TLS on, do steps 2 and 3, then set SASL to your nick and password. Alumni and guests: ask in #help and an admin will let you in.

Channels

#general introductions and announcements
#organizing meetings and campus organizing
#cs computer science (more)
#help questions about the network
#offtopic everything else

Anyone with an account can make a new channel by joining it.

Logs

A bot saves messages in every channel above except #offtopic, so decisions don't get lost. The log isn't published. Private messages are never saved.
