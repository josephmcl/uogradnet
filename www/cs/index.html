#cs

The channel for computer science grad students at UO. Deadlines, TA questions, reading groups, cluster outages, job leads. Everyone is welcome.

Join #cs in your browser, or connect to irc.uogradnet.xyz, port 6767, with TLS on, and type /join #cs.

You'll need an account registered with your uoregon.edu address, and #cs is logged. uogradnet.xyz explains both.
